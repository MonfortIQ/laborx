How to Prepare for a Fasting Blood Sugar Test

Learn how to prepare for a fasting blood sugar test, including timing, medication questions, and appointment guidance.

Diet & Nutrition
Oct 08, 2026 - 4 min readReviewed content

Following collection instructions for a fasting glucose test helps make the result easier for a qualified clinician to interpret.

Before your appointment

Follow the fasting duration shown with your booking. Water is usually allowed, but confirm the exact instruction for your selected test or package.

Medication and health context

If you take regular medication or have diabetes care instructions, ask your clinician whether any changes are needed before the test.
