Robots in the Lab

Behind the scenes of modern diagnostic centers, a quiet revolution is happening. Advanced robotic tracks now handle the movement of test tubes, centrifugation, and aliquoting.

Speed and Accuracy

Automation eliminates human error in sample handling and significantly accelerates turnaround times. A test that historically took 24 hours can now be processed, verified by AI, and delivered to your phone in under 6 hours.

The Role of AI

While machines handle the physical movement, AI assists pathologists in reviewing cellular abnormalities, acting as a tireless second set of eyes to ensure nothing is missed.
