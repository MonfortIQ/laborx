Predictive Analytics

We are generating more health data than ever before. But data without context is useless. The future of healthcare lies in predictive analytics—using historical data to forecast future health risks.

Shifting from Reactive to Proactive

Currently, medicine is largely reactive: you get sick, you take a test. By analyzing millions of data points, predictive models can identify subtle biomarker trends that indicate a disease process is starting long before symptoms appear.

Your Digital Twin

In the near future, your continuous stream of diagnostic and lifestyle data will power a 'digital twin'—a virtual model of your biology that doctors can use to simulate treatments and optimize your longevity.
