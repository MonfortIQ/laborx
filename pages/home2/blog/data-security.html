Securing Your Personal Health Data

4 min Read
Dr. Robert Cline Chief Medical Officer, LaboraX

Your Most Private Asset

Your medical data is your most private asset. As we digitize healthcare, the responsibility to protect this data from cyber threats becomes our absolute highest priority.

End-to-End Encryption

We utilize military-grade end-to-end encryption for all patient records. This means that your data is encrypted on your device and can only be decrypted by authorized medical professionals with whom you explicitly share access.

Immutable Audit Logs

Using blockchain technology, we maintain an immutable audit log of your health vault. You can see exactly who accessed your records, when, and for what purpose, giving you total oversight and control.
