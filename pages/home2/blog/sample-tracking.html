Why Real-time Sample Tracking Matters

Bringing transparency and trust to the digital diagnostic testing process.

3 min Read
Written By Dr. Kevin Hart

The Anxiety of Waiting

In healthcare, waiting is often the hardest part. Patients frequently experience anxiety wondering if their sample arrived safely at the laboratory, or when they will get their results.

Logistics Meets Diagnostics

By applying modern supply-chain logistics to clinical pathology, we can eliminate this anxiety. GPS-tracked phlebotomists and barcoded sample tubes mean you can track your blood test exactly like you track a pizza delivery.

Quality Control

Beyond patient peace of mind, real-time tracking ensures strict quality control. Temperature sensors on transport cases alert the lab instantly if a sample deviates from the required temperature range, guaranteeing test accuracy.
