Clinical Overview

A Kidney Function Test (KFT) panel checks if your kidneys are functioning properly by measuring the levels of various substances, including several minerals, electrolytes, proteins, and glucose (sugar), in the blood.

Parameters Included

  • Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN)
  • Serum Creatinine
  • Uric Acid
  • Electrolytes (Sodium, Potassium, Chloride)
No fasting required, though it is often done alongside fasting tests.
