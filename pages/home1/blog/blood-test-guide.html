Understanding Your Complete Blood Count

A simple guide to deciphering what your CBC results mean for your health.

What is a CBC?

A Complete Blood Count (CBC) is one of the most common blood tests ordered by doctors. It measures several components and features of your blood, including red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets.

Why is it important?

Your blood is a window into your health. Abnormal increases or decreases in cell counts as revealed in a CBC may indicate that you have an underlying medical condition that calls for further evaluation.

How to prepare

For a standard CBC, you can eat and drink normally. However, if your doctor has ordered a CBC alongside a lipid panel or fasting glucose test, you will need to fast.
