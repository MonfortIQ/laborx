The Silent Epidemic

Type 2 diabetes often develops slowly. In fact, many people have prediabetes or diabetes for years without knowing it, because the early symptoms can be so mild.

The HbA1c Test

The A1C test—also known as the hemoglobin A1C or HbA1c test—is a simple blood test that measures your average blood sugar levels over the past 3 months. It's one of the commonly used tests to diagnose prediabetes and diabetes.

When to test?

If you are over 45, or if you have risk factors like a family history of diabetes, being overweight, or a sedentary lifestyle, the ADA recommends getting a baseline screening.
