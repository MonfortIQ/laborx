How to Prepare for Your Blood Test

A simple guide to deciphering what your CBC results mean for your health.

Getting a blood test is a standard part of routine healthcare, but the steps you take in the hours leading up to your appointment can significantly impact the accuracy of your results. Proper preparation ensures that your doctor gets the most reliable data possible.

1. Follow Fasting Instructions Strictly

Many tests, especially Lipid Panels (cholesterol) and Fasting Glucose tests, require you to abstain from all food and beverages (except water) for 8 to 12 hours before the draw. Even a splash of milk in your morning coffee or a stick of gum can alter your blood sugar and triglyceride levels, invalidating the test.

"When in doubt, drink water. Staying hydrated makes it much easier for the phlebotomist to locate your veins and draw blood painlessly."

2. Manage Your Medications

Unless instructed otherwise by your physician, you should continue taking your prescribed medications. However, it is crucial to inform the lab technician about any supplements, vitamins, or over-the-counter drugs you are taking, as things like Biotin (Vitamin B7) can severely interfere with thyroid test results.

3. Avoid Strenuous Exercise

Heavy workouts right before a blood test can alter your enzyme levels (like creatine kinase) and affect your electrolyte balance. It's best to stick to light activity or rest in the 24 hours preceding a comprehensive metabolic panel.
